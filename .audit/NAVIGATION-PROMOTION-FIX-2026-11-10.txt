Navigation and Kargo promotion form fixes - 2026-11-10

Changes
- Esc restores the actual entry point rather than a hardcoded view hierarchy.
  Each Argo frame saves scopes, filters, explicit Space selections, stable
  application identity, cursor and scroll. Parent resource trees retain their
  expanded nodes and resource cursor. Context picker and Kargo preserve their
  Argo entry points. Manually opened events return to the original resource.
- Removed the global Escape debounce and the extra selection-clearing back
  step. All-context action models return straight to their overview, including
  cancellation of an operation dialog, without an intermediate context list.
- Enter and :app move/open the cursor target without creating checkbox
  selections. Only explicitly checked overview applications are copied into
  action models. Application identity includes context and metadata namespace.
- Kargo records the actual Project/Stage/Promotion/Warehouse/Freight/instance
  entry points, their filters and cursor targets, including detail pagers.
- Ctrl+P opens a colored promotion form with fixed instance/project and source
  Stage or Freight. Arrows choose the other field; typing/paste filters locally.
  Tab / Shift+Tab moves through the choice, Cancel and Promote; Enter activates
  the focused button. Ctrl+P remains available as a submission shortcut.
- Retry uses Cancel/Retry buttons and preserves its list or describe/YAML
  origin. Cancelling or returning from a result restores the original object.
- The form loads only the missing scoped list, reuses its short-lived cache,
  and never automatically replays writes. Responses are gated by active view,
  epoch, request sequence, exact target and session identity. Changed targets,
  empty choices and repeated submission while executing cannot issue a POST.
